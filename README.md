# SKOOLchat for Android

**Your school. Your conversations.**

SKOOLchat is for chatting with your friends at school. This is the Android app: open it
from your home screen like any other app, with no browser tabs and no address bar.

Website: https://beyou.figma.site

---

## Download

**[Download SKOOLchat.apk](../../releases/latest/download/SKOOLchat.apk)**

You can also find it on the **Releases** page on the right side of this repository.

## Install

1. Open the download on your Android phone (tap the file in your notifications or in the **Files** / **Downloads** app).
2. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or Files app. Tap **Settings**, switch on **Allow from this source**, then go back.
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

**Seeing a "Play Protect" warning?** That's normal for apps that don't come from the
Play Store. Tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**.
